\section*{1\quad Ruleout and Outcome-Transformation Design}

For each saved cohort panel, observations are grouped by company and product
(\texttt{BoardName} $\times$ \texttt{product}). Using all observed quarters in
that cohort file, the ruleout score for company $b$, product $p$, and cohort $c$
is
\[
R_{bpc}
=
\frac{\max(\texttt{price1}_{bpc})/\min(\texttt{price1}_{bpc})}
     {\max(\texttt{price0}_{bpc})/\min(\texttt{price0}_{bpc})}.
\]
For a threshold $\tau$, every row of the company--product history is removed
from that cohort when $R_{bpc}>\tau$; equality is retained. The score uses the
original price levels. After removal, a cohort with no remaining treated
observations is not stacked.

This report deliberately combines the original benchmark with the new
no-winsorization runs. Ruleout 0 is the same benchmark used in the previous
report: no ruleout deletion, followed by upper-p95 winsorization of
\texttt{price1} and then the log transformation. For thresholds 50, 10, and 5,
the retained original \texttt{price1} values are logged directly, with no
winsorization or trimming. Thus, comparisons with ruleout 0 reflect both the
ruleout deletion and the removal of winsorization. Comparisons among thresholds
50, 10, and 5 differ only in the threshold and its resulting sample.

Treatment status, control status, event timing, event cohort, and dynamic ATC3
sharing are read from the same saved cohort files and are not reconstructed by
ruleout. All other settings are common: the
\texttt{drop\_sentinel\_yearquarter} roster, \texttt{ever\_board=1},
\texttt{ieoc\_ref=1}, \texttt{late=3}, new requirement, stay, and sharing-time
rules, dynamic ATC3 sharing, \texttt{price1}, req2, the Not control group,
company--product--cohort fixed effects, cohort--calendar-quarter fixed effects,
company clustering, horizons 0--7, three pre-event coefficients, and no Kappa,
other-event, or ATC3 fixed-effect controls.
